## What we collect

**When you create an account**
Your name, email address, phone number and date of birth. If you sign in with
Google or Facebook instead of a password, we receive and store the access tokens
that let us recognise you on return visits. If you set a password, we store it
hashed — never as readable text. You may add a profile picture.

**When you sign in**
The IP address and browser user-agent of the device you signed in from, kept
with your session so we can secure your account.

**When you shop**
Your shipping and billing addresses, the contents of your cart, the items on
your wishlist, and your order history. When you place an order we save a _copy_
of the delivery address onto the order itself, so your order records stay
correct even if you later edit or delete the saved address.

**When you pay**
We do **not** receive, see or store your card number. Card payments are handled
by Stripe, and your card details go directly to them. What we keep is the
transaction reference Stripe returns, the amount, the currency and whether the
payment succeeded. If you pay cash on delivery there is no card data at all.

**When you write a review**
Your review text and rating. Reviews are **published publicly on the product
page alongside your name**, so do not include anything in a review you would not
want other shoppers to read.

**When you contact us or subscribe**
Your messages to us, and your email address if you sign up for our newsletter.

**Customer records**
We keep a customer record keyed to your phone number so that repeat orders,
loyalty totals and any notes our staff add about your orders stay together in
one place.

## Why we hold it, and on what basis

- **To perform our contract with you** — processing, packing, delivering and
  invoicing your order, handling returns and refunds, and contacting you about
  an order.
- **Because the law requires it** — keeping accounting and tax records.
- **With your consent** — marketing email, which you can withdraw at any time
  using the unsubscribe link in any message, or by contacting us. We also use
  your date of birth to send you a birthday offer.
- **For our legitimate interest in running the store** — securing accounts,
  preventing fraud, and understanding which products sell.

## Cookies

This store sets a cookie that keeps you signed in and remembers your cart. It is
essential to the site working, and there is nothing to opt out of because we do
not run advertising pixels, analytics trackers or third-party marketing scripts
on this site.

## Who your data reaches

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed, with:

- **Our delivery courier** — your name, address and phone, so your order can be
  delivered.
- **Stripe** — payment processing.
- **Resend** — sending order confirmations and account email.
- **UploadThing** — storing product and profile images.
- **Neon** — our database.
- **Upstash** — rate limiting that protects the sign-in form.
- **Vercel** — hosting.
- **Google and Facebook** — only if you choose to sign in with them.

## How long we keep it

Order, payment and invoice records are kept for as long as accounting and tax
law requires. Your account and the data attached to it are kept until you ask us
to delete them. Marketing subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy
- correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
- delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it
- stop using your data for marketing, at any time
- withdraw a consent you previously gave
- object to a particular use of your data

You can delete your account yourself from your account settings. When you do,
your orders remain in our records as anonymous entries, because tax law requires
us to keep the transaction — but they are no longer linked to you.

To exercise any of these rights, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. If you are not
satisfied with our answer, you may complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection
Centre, or to the Consumer Protection Agency on **19588**.

## Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Card details never reach our systems. Access to
customer records is limited to staff who need it for their work.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes
will be brought to your attention rather than made quietly.
