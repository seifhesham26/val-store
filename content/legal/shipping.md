## Responsibility for your parcel

We remain responsible for your order until it is delivered to you, or to
somebody you have authorised to receive it on your behalf. We use courier
companies to carry out delivery, but using a courier does not transfer that
responsibility away from us.

If your parcel arrives damaged, do not accept it if the damage is visible at the
door — and either way, contact us straight away. Items damaged before they reach
you are covered by the 30-day right described on our
[Returns & Exchanges](/returns) page, and we pay the return cost in that case.
