## How long do I have to return something?

You have 14 days from the day your order reaches you to return an item without
giving a reason. If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, you have 30
days. The two are separate rights — see [Returns & Exchanges](/returns) for the
detail.

## Who pays for return shipping?

If you are returning something because you changed your mind, you do. If the
item is faulty, damaged, wrong, or not as described, we do — once we have
inspected it and confirmed the problem.

## How do I get my refund?

Your refund goes back to the same method you paid with. Card payments return to
the card you used. For cash-on-delivery orders there is no card to refund, so we
arrange it with you directly. We cannot give you store credit instead of a
refund you are entitled to in money.

## Do you ship outside Egypt?

No. We deliver within Egypt only.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Visa and Mastercard, processed securely by Stripe, and cash on delivery. Your
card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by us.

## How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched you will receive tracking details by
[TRACKING METHOD]. You can also check the status of any order from your account.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible. If your order has not been dispatched yet we can
usually change or cancel it. Once it has been dispatched, your return rights
apply instead.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Not yet. Orders arrive in our standard packaging.
